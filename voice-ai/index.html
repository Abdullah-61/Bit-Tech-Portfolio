How we deliver

  • Discover leads: Apollo, LinkedIn, and map sources provide candidate businesses.
  • Enrich with context: ScrapeGraphAI/Fathom add business-specific details for personalization.
  • Run voice calls: VAPI + Twilio execute outbound conversations with STT/LLM/TTS loop.
  • Track and follow up: SSE progress, transcript logs, and prioritization feed campaign actions.

How we deliver

  • Curate legal sources as DOCX documents with optional article metadata and series information.
  • Collect writing controls including tone, custom tones, firm identity, location, and SEO keywords.
  • Generate first draft through an Azure Function endpoint backed by Azure OpenAI.
  • Iterate safely with chat-style edits and manual adjustments before finalization.
  • Add visual support through on-demand AI image generation for the same post.
  • Ship final output as formatted DOCX for marketing and publishing workflows.

Platform capabilities

Account & profile management

Tone governance

Built-in writing styles plus user-defined custom tones stored per account for repeatable output.

Structured legal source library

Conversational revision loop
